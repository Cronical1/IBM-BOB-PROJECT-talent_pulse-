1. Problem
Top 3-4 enterprise pain points
  • Severe Turnover Costs: Unplanned employee departures cost 50%-200% of annual salary per hire ($30k-$150k).
  • Reactive Detection: HR finds out about flight risk only when a resignation letter is handed in.
  • Career Stagnation: Lack of customized upskilling and promotion paths leads to employee burnout and disengagement.
Existing Alternatives
  • Annual employee pulse surveys
  • Exit interview questionnaires
  • Static HR dashboard reports (Excel, Tableau)
2. Solution
Top 3 features transforming the problem
  • Dual ML Predictive Intelligence: Random Forest Classifier for attrition probability + Gradient Boosting Regressor for performance evaluation.
  • Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution: Identifies individual attrition drivers (overtime, comp ratio, promotion plateau).
  • Autonomous Multi-Agent Copilot: Generates manager stay-interview scripts, retention packages, and tailored IBM SkillsBuild learning pathways.
Key Metrics
  • Attrition Prediction Recall & ROC-AUC
  • Reduction in unplanned quarterly turnover (%)
  • Employee tenure extension (average months)
  • HR manager triage time saved per case
